# Prep Book

Fady's recipe and meal-prep app: 52 gym-friendly recipes from his saved posts, a week planner, a shopping list grouped by the cheapest store, grocery prices refreshed every other week, and a hands-free cook mode with timers.

Open the site in Safari on an iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It runs full screen and works offline. The plan, shopping list and timers are saved on the phone.

prices.json is replaced by the scheduled price check. Recipes and photos belong to the creators credited on each recipe page.
